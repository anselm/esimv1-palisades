This is one run of the Palisades fire on Rothermel's spread equations, from the morning of 7 January 2025 to 12 January. Drag the bar under the map to move through the five days, or press play. Everything on this page follows the moment the bar shows.

This model spreads fire by Rothermel's equations, the ones behind the fire-behaviour tools agencies use: a rate of spread for each cell from its fuel, its slope, the wind and the dryness of the fuel.

The fuel model assigned to each cell

Fire agencies mapped the edge of the fire about once a day. At each mapping the model's burned area is compared with the mapped one, and the structures the model's fire reached with those inspectors recorded destroyed.

On the reanalysis wind the model is about eight times too slow through the first 27 hours and ends the fourth day near the mapped size. The gusts variant is faster early and overruns by the end.

The weather is one point of the ERA5 reanalysis, hour by hour, for the whole window.

  • The fuel table is recalled from memory of Scott and Burgan (2005) and has not been checked against it.
  • The wind is one point of a coarse reanalysis and does not bend around the terrain.
  • Nobody fights the fire unless the variant says so.
  • This model writes no elevation frame, so its run page has a flat map where the other fire pages have a relief.
  • The first mapped perimeter is a hand-drawn triangle.
